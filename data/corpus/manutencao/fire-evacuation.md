# Fire evacuation procedure

## Purpose
Evacuate guests and staff safely and account for everyone at the assembly point.

## Alarm signals
Continuous siren: full evacuation.
Intermittent signal: standby and prepare to evacuate.

## Immediate actions on discovery of fire
1. Raise the alarm using the nearest call point.
2. Call reception and report the location.
3. Close doors behind you as you leave.
4. Do not use the lifts.
5. Use the nearest marked emergency exit.

## Evacuation of guests
Knock on every door on the way out.
Assist guests with reduced mobility using the designated refuge areas.
Direct guests to the outdoor assembly point in the north car park.

## Assembly point
The assembly point is the north car park, 60 metres from the building.
Floor supervisors report head counts to the fire warden.

## Fire warden duties
Confirm that all floors are clear.
Meet the fire brigade at the main entrance.
Provide the building plan and the list of missing persons.

## Prohibitions
Never re-enter the building until the fire brigade authorises it.
Never use the lifts during an evacuation.
Never block emergency exits with equipment or luggage.

## Drills
A full evacuation drill is held twice a year.
All new staff receive evacuation training during induction.
